# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

This plugin, SEO Page Structure Checklist, is an unofficial developer checklist for Claude Code and Claude apps.

## Data the plugin handles

- The plugin does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. It has no server and no database.
- The plugin does not store the pages you audit or the questions you ask. Content you provide during a session is processed only within that session, under the rules of the Claude product you use.
- The plugin's skills instruct Claude to fetch two kinds of URLs: pages on `developers.google.com` (the Google Search Central documentation) and the public page you asked to audit. Fetched content is read as data and is never executed.

## Retention

The plugin itself retains nothing. Any retention of session content is governed by the Claude product you use, not by this plugin.

## Children

The plugin is not directed at users under 18.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published in this repository, and the "Last updated" date is revised.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in https://github.com/1abcdefggs/seo-page-structure-checklist.
